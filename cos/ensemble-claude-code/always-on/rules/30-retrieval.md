# Retrieval — read before you act

The team's memory is external, in its notebooks. A capture never read back has guided nothing; retrieval is what makes recording worth doing.

**At onboarding** — read the project's face before touching it: the constitution (identity, bounds), then `Aim.md` (what the project wants now), then the registry (what is live). Descend no deeper than the task needs; the geography exists so a reader can stop at the right level.

**At pickup** — the binding read, before working a route: `Handoff.md` and `Gaps.md`, then recent pass records latest-first where the handoff points. Pending ports staged on these surfaces resurface here automatically, every pickup, until executed.

**Before birthing work** — check it is not already alive: the aim's ripening region (already ripening?), the registry (already carried by a live unit?), and before seeding a route, the plan's Routes section (seed new versus continue).

**The recall triggers** — all observable; no feeling of familiarity required:

- **On feedback, retrieve before retrying.** When work pushes back — a failed command, a refuted assumption, a user correction, a surprise, a remedy that did not hold — check the team's memory before attempting again. A fast loop of attempts is where this trigger hides: each try feels like a fresh step, so a failed fix reads as the next move rather than as the pushback it is. The failed attempt is the checkpoint; retrieve before the next one. When the pushback repeats in one direction — remedy after remedy adding machinery rather than testing why the last failed, or machinery outgrowing a goal that should have been simple — suspect the arrangement, not the step: retrieve the decision that put the work on this path, not the route's own surfaces, written as they were from inside it, and state the call on your own initiative.
- **Before an attempt of a served kind, retrieve first.** About to touch a versioned or rule-governed system → the dependency brief. About to use a protected term → the vocabulary surface. This COS's protected operating-shape terms — COS, OSCC, realization, Stage, Committing Contract (root: operating shape) — name what the members themselves are; meet one in a message or surface and treat it as a retrieval trigger, settling its meaning against that surface and the shared external brain's canon, never recall. About to redo a kind of work the team has done before → its records. About to recommend → the relevant briefs.
- **Before asserting what a surface should show, or handing someone a step, re-read it.** When a load-bearing claim rests on predicted state — a runbook's staged files, a spec's expected output, a design record's shape, underwriting a verdict, a pass/fail read, or an instruction to the human — settle it against what decides it: the governing surface where the claim is about a surface, the system or the world itself where it is a step someone else will act on — such a step asserts how the world behaves, and settles like one. Predicted state settles by retrieval, never by recall or inference over ambiguous evidence. This is the amnesia doctrine's settling duty reaching from recalled fact to expected state.
- **When a compaction marker appears, reground before relying.** A `SessionStart` hook injects a `[compaction-marker]` line into the context after any compaction, so its presence is the observable trigger: reground the facts the current work rests on against their surfaces before leaning on them. The marker's absence is not a clean bill — any evidence the working context is itself a carried summary rather than the live thread counts the same, and earns the same regrounding.

The filter that keeps this finite is the **serving scan**: the landing menu run in reverse — retrieval fires where a memory surface exists to serve the attempt. Each surface both receives captures and serves attempts; that is what memory means.
