# The work-object model — how every member individuates work

Work is deliberate happening toward betterness. Every member cuts work into the same units; nobody invents a private work model.

**Two shapes of work.** A **pursuit** closes a gap: it has an acceptance, and meeting it discharges it. A **tending** holds or raises a valued condition: it has a standard (checkable kept-conditions) plus a trigger rule, and it ends only by retirement decision, never by achievement. One beat of a tending is an **occurrence** — the tending's own beat, not new work; it spawns a pursuit only when it uncovers gap-closing work bigger than the beat.

**Exactly five unit kinds** — a closed list; never mint new kinds: **project** (a front of deliberate work around one **body** — the thing the work is about), **pursuit**, **tending**, **route** (one way toward a pursuit's destination; a changed way is a new route, never an edited one), **iteration** (one pickup-to-stop push along a route; a pass ends by stopping, and empty-handed passes are still recorded).

**How units nest.** **Scoping** is lifecycle-bound membership: the scoped unit retires when its host closes. **Spawning** is origin without lifecycle-binding: the spawned unit closes on its own. Routes belong to their pursuit by definition, iterations to their route; a route never finishes by achievement — it ends with its pursuit or is retired by decision.

**Designation.** Every unit is designated — declared as one named thing — with five slots: identity, aim, placement, notebook, bounds; designation always follows an acceptance decision. The durable kinds carry an instrument: a **contract** designates a pursuit (singular per pursuit, living, refined inline, never re-minted; its spec elaborates the destination, its plan the path; thin kernel first, then elaborate). A **charter** designates a tending (standard, trigger rule, bounds — what a beat may do alone versus flag-and-spawn — and a method elaborating into a runbook). A **constitution** designates a project (no path facet: the registry roster is the living path). Routes and iterations carry a **thin designation** instead: all five slots, lightly, at the head of the unit's own notebook. Every designation is filed twice: the founding surface of the unit's own notebook, plus a registry line in the parent's notebook.

**Notebooks.** One notebook per designated work unit; a unit and its notebook are born together. A notebook is a maintained current-state surface: keep it the present truth, prune what stopped being true; chronology is git's job, and commit discipline is a notebook obligation (dated check results and occurrence records stay as content where the current truth includes history). When a unit ends, its notebook freezes into preserved evidence — never maintained again, never deleted. Notebook surfaces never nest inside the body's own surfaces — a `docs/` folder is body documentation, part of the body, never a notebook home. In a project the ensemble did not found — adoption of an existing body — the notebook geography is still declared explicitly in the constitution's notebook declaration at designation, defaulting to a single `notebook/` folder at the body's root: that folder is the project's own notebook, holding its face — constitution, aim surface, registry — with the units' own notebooks nested inside it as the units themselves nest. A route's notebook is its folder at `routes/<route-name>/` (`Handoff.md` with the thin designation at its head, `Gaps.md`, `iterations/`); an iteration's is its own record file, opened at pickup, frozen at the stop. Small notebooks collapse their geography into their own structure — never manufacture extra index or registry files inside route folders.

**Effects.** An effect is a trace a pass leaves — an insight, a clarification, a produced thing, a record. An effect guides nothing unless it lands in a notebook.

**Where new work comes from.** The aim generates, the body accumulates: new pursuits and tendings are born from the project's aim surface (`Aim.md`: founding wish, body-state asymptote, ripening region), never straight from the body's parts list. The second honest birth path is spawning from live work — provenance recorded, a one-sentence acceptance in the moment, no queueing in the aim.
