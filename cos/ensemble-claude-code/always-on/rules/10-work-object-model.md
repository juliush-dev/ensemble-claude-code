# The work-object model — how every member individuates work

Happening is the root; work is happening specified as deliberate and toward betterness. Every member cuts work into the same units; nobody invents a private work model.

**Two shapes of work.** A **pursuit** closes a gap: it has an acceptance, and meeting it discharges it. A **tending** holds or raises a valued condition: it has a standard (checkable kept-conditions) plus a trigger rule, and it ends only by retirement decision, never by achievement. One beat of a tending is an **occurrence** — the tending's own beat, not new work; it spawns a pursuit only when it uncovers gap-closing work bigger than the beat.

**Exactly five unit kinds** — a closed list; never mint new kinds: **project** (a front of deliberate work around one **body** — the thing the work is about), **pursuit**, **tending**, **route** (one way toward a pursuit's destination; a changed way is a new route, never an edited one), **iteration** (one pickup-to-stop push along a route; a pass ends by stopping, and empty-handed passes are still recorded).

**How units nest.** **Scoping** is lifecycle-bound membership: the scoped unit retires when its host closes. **Spawning** is origin without lifecycle-binding: the spawned unit closes on its own. Routes belong to their pursuit by definition, iterations to their route; a route never finishes by achievement — it ends with its pursuit or is retired by decision.

**Designation.** Every unit is designated after an acceptance decision, declared as one named thing with five slots (identity, aim, placement, notebook, bounds) and filed twice, as its own notebook's founding surface and as a registry line in its parent's: a **contract** designates a pursuit, a **charter** a tending, a **constitution** a project, and routes and iterations carry a **thin designation** at the head of their own notebook; `designate` carries each instrument.

**The project anatomy.** A project is a folder holding three repositories — the ensemble makes sure each is one: `notebook/`, its own notebook, holding the face (constitution, aim surface, registry) and beneath it `pursuits/` and `tendings/`, each unit's instrument in its own folder, routes and iterations nesting as the units nest; `body/`, the thing the work is about, no prescribed inner shape; `inbox/`, incoming, not-yet-triaged material, neither body nor notebook until a decision routes it, and nothing leaves it on its own. This is the shape of every project the ensemble works in, founded or adopted; putting it around a project is wrapping it, and the ensemble works only inside a wrapped project. A multi-body project is a faceless grouping folder, each sub-project carrying the full shape; `wrap` carries how groupings nest.

**Notebooks.** One notebook per designated work unit; a unit and its notebook are born together. A notebook is a maintained current-state surface: keep it the present truth, prune what stopped being true; chronology is git's job, and commit discipline is a notebook obligation (dated check results and occurrence records stay as content where the current truth includes history). When a unit ends, its notebook freezes into preserved evidence — never maintained again, never deleted. Notebook surfaces never nest inside the body's own surfaces — a `docs/` folder is body documentation, part of the body, never a notebook home. And of everything this model names, only the body ever leaves the project: deploys, publishes, and hand-offs carry the body and nothing else; all else stays home, moving only between the team's own memory surfaces (ports do this) — the human's own deliberate exports on his word are named as such; the law targets silent or default leaving. Its other face, in a project shared with other contributors: what the ensemble pushes is a projection — the project exactly in its shared form, conforming to the shared anatomy, nothing of the wrap in it — and what each contributor wraps the project in is their business alone, never collective. Route and iteration notebooks are laid out in `pass-discipline`.

**Project health.** A project's health is the project and its goal kept whole from every atom up to the whole, considered collectively, and every happening in it is aware of and unfolds to protect that health, a standing stance of every act, never a check. It also smells wrap needs (fusion, absorption, renaming, restructuring, and the residue a quick decision leaves), raising a wrap-discussion suggestion the human alone opens; what health is, its two common metrics and the beat run only on the human's order live in `health-check`.

**Effects.** An effect is a trace a pass leaves — an insight, a clarification, a produced thing, a record. An effect guides nothing unless it lands in a notebook.

**Where new work comes from.** The aim generates, the body accumulates: new pursuits and tendings are born from the project's aim surface (`Aim.md`: founding wish, body-state asymptote, ripening region), never straight from the body's parts list. The second honest birth path is spawning from live work — provenance recorded, a one-sentence acceptance in the moment, no queueing in the aim.
