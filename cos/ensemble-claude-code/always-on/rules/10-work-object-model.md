# The work-object model — how every member individuates work

Happening is the root; work is happening specified as deliberate and toward betterness. Every member cuts work into the same units; nobody invents a private work model.

**Two shapes of work.** A **pursuit** closes a gap: it has an acceptance, and meeting it discharges it. A **tending** holds or raises a valued condition: it has a standard (checkable kept-conditions) plus a trigger rule, and it ends only by retirement decision, never by achievement. One beat of a tending is an **occurrence** — the tending's own beat, not new work; it spawns a pursuit only when it uncovers gap-closing work bigger than the beat.

**Exactly five unit kinds** — a closed list; never mint new kinds: **project** (a front of deliberate work around one **body** — the thing the work is about), **pursuit**, **tending**, **route** (one way toward a pursuit's destination; a changed way is a new route, never an edited one), **iteration** (one pickup-to-stop push along a route; a pass ends by stopping, and empty-handed passes are still recorded).

**How units nest.** **Scoping** is lifecycle-bound membership: the scoped unit retires when its host closes. **Spawning** is origin without lifecycle-binding: the spawned unit closes on its own. Routes belong to their pursuit by definition, iterations to their route; a route never finishes by achievement — it ends with its pursuit or is retired by decision.

**Designation.** Every unit is designated — declared as one named thing — with five slots: identity, aim, placement, notebook, bounds; designation always follows an acceptance decision. The durable kinds carry an instrument: a **contract** designates a pursuit (singular per pursuit, living, refined inline, never re-minted; its spec elaborates the destination, its plan the path; thin kernel first, then elaborate). A **charter** designates a tending (standard, trigger rule, bounds — what a beat may do alone versus flag-and-spawn — and a method elaborating into a runbook). A **constitution** designates a project (no path facet: the registry roster is the living path). Routes and iterations carry a **thin designation** instead: all five slots, lightly, at the head of the unit's own notebook. Every designation is filed twice: the founding surface of the unit's own notebook, plus a registry line in the parent's notebook.

**The project anatomy.** A project is a folder holding three repositories — the ensemble makes sure each is one: `notebook/`, its own notebook, holding the face (constitution, aim surface, registry) and beneath it `pursuits/` and `tendings/`, each unit's instrument in its own folder, routes and iterations nesting as the units nest; `body/`, the thing the work is about, no prescribed inner shape; `inbox/`, incoming, not-yet-triaged material, neither body nor notebook until a decision routes it, and nothing leaves it on its own. This is the shape of every project the ensemble works in, founded or adopted; putting it around a project is wrapping it, and the ensemble works only inside a wrapped project. A multi-body project is a pure grouping folder — no notebook, body, or face of its own — each sub-project carrying the full shape; why they are grouped is deduced from the group's content, never recorded.

**Notebooks.** One notebook per designated work unit; a unit and its notebook are born together. A notebook is a maintained current-state surface: keep it the present truth, prune what stopped being true; chronology is git's job, and commit discipline is a notebook obligation (dated check results and occurrence records stay as content where the current truth includes history). When a unit ends, its notebook freezes into preserved evidence — never maintained again, never deleted. Notebook surfaces never nest inside the body's own surfaces — a `docs/` folder is body documentation, part of the body, never a notebook home. And of everything this model names, only the body ever leaves the project: deploys, publishes, and hand-offs carry the body and nothing else; all else stays home, moving only between the team's own memory surfaces (ports do this) — the human's own deliberate exports on his word are named as such; the law targets silent or default leaving. Its other face, in a project shared with other contributors: what the ensemble pushes is a projection — the project exactly in its shared form, conforming to the shared anatomy, nothing of the wrap in it — and what each contributor wraps the project in is their business alone, never collective. A route's notebook is its folder at `routes/<route-name>/` (`Handoff.md` with the thin designation at its head, `Gaps.md`, `iterations/`); an iteration's is its own record file, opened at pickup, frozen at the stop. Small notebooks collapse their geography into their own structure — never manufacture extra index or registry files inside route folders.

**Project health.** The subject of health is the project and its goal, ensured throughout its existence by everything in place, from every atom — the smallest meaningful element, content or container — up to the whole, considered collectively, not individually: unhealth enters through one atom, or collectively from parts sound on their own. The ensemble sees every project this way: a thing is what it contributes, statically (as on disk) and empirically (as lived — executed, read and understood, perceived, felt or seen by an agentic being, human or AI, or all four together); meaning and unhealth arise collectively, bottom-up, in interaction with the beings who live them. Two metrics are common to every project and deepen project-specifically: story-consistency — the present coherent account of what each atom contributes, statically and empirically, to the meaning that makes the project one coherent, purposeful thing that can be thought, discussed, spoken, and worked about; goal-alignment — the same constituents showing, by the structure of their existence, that they exist for the goal, not against it. Two guardians: every happening in a project is aware of, and unfolds to protect, its health — a standing stance of every act, never a check; and a health-check tending, run only on the human's order. A multi-body project bears health as a whole; each sub-project owns its own, the parent's the collective.

**Effects.** An effect is a trace a pass leaves — an insight, a clarification, a produced thing, a record. An effect guides nothing unless it lands in a notebook.

**Where new work comes from.** The aim generates, the body accumulates: new pursuits and tendings are born from the project's aim surface (`Aim.md`: founding wish, body-state asymptote, ripening region), never straight from the body's parts list. The second honest birth path is spawning from live work — provenance recorded, a one-sentence acceptance in the moment, no queueing in the aim.
