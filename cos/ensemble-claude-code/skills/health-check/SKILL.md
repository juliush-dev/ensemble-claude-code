# Health check — the ordered beat on a project's health

Project health is the standing stance of every act (`10-work-object-model.md`); this runbook is its other guardian — a tending's beat, run only when the human orders it, never unordered. One such tending per project, chartered at wrap time.

## The charter, minimal

- **Standard:** the two common kept-conditions — story-consistency and goal-alignment, as the work-object model states them — plus the project's own deeper ones, declared in the charter as the project's health comes to be understood.
- **Trigger rule:** the human's order, and nothing else.
- **Bounds:** a beat reads, judges, and records; it repairs nothing on its own — what it finds is flagged, and what exceeds the beat spawns a pursuit.
- **Notebook:** the project's `notebook/tendings/health-check/`.

## The beat

1. Read the charter (the `occurrence` runbook's before-the-beat read): confirm the order that fired it; take the standard, the project's own conditions included.
2. Sweep from the atoms up: every atom — the smallest meaningful element, content or container — read for what it contributes, statically (as on disk) and empirically (as lived: executed, read and understood, perceived, felt or seen). Then the whole, considered collectively: parts sound alone can still be unhealthy together.
3. Judge condition by condition, never as an impression. Story-consistency: does the present account of what each atom contributes cohere into one purposeful thing that can be thought, discussed, spoken, and worked about? Goal-alignment: do the constituents show, by the structure of their existence, that they exist for the goal and not against it? Then the project's own conditions, each the same way.
4. At a grouping folder: one beat per sub-project against its own charter, a nested grouping (no charter of its own) recursed into instead, its health the collective of its children's; then the collective read of the parent — the group's health being the collective one — recorded with the group.

## The record, and what exceeds the beat

Write the occurrence record per the `occurrence` runbook: when, and on whose order; what was swept; the verdict per kept-condition — held or not, with what decided it; effects kept; spawns linked. A null result is recorded. Every finding lands on a maintained surface by the menu scan — a `Gaps.md`, the aim's ripening region, the constitution's bounds — never only in the return. Repair bigger than the beat spawns a pursuit through the `designate` runbook, provenance recorded; a fusion, absorption, renaming, or restructuring need is named to the human as a wrap-discussion suggestion per the `wrap` runbook, never acted on.
