# Occurrence — a tending's beat

A tending holds or raises a valued condition; an occurrence is one beat of it — one rescan, one grooming pass, one check. The beat is the tending's own work, not a new unit.

## Before the beat

Read the charter: the **standard** (the checkable kept-conditions this beat judges against), the **trigger rule** (confirm this firing matches it — which trigger fired?), and the **bounds** (what a beat may do alone versus what it must flag-and-spawn).

## The beat

Do the method's work — the charter's method facet, elaborated in this very runbook family or in a tending-specific runbook where one exists. Judge the result **against the standard**, condition by checkable condition, never as a general impression.

## Record the occurrence — null results always

Every beat writes its occurrence record into the tending's notebook (the maintained brief plus dated check results as chronology-as-content):

- **When fired, and which trigger.**
- **What was done.**
- **Verdict against the standard** — which kept-conditions hold, which do not.
- **Effects kept** — what changed in the world or the notebooks, landed where.
- **Spawns linked** — any pursuit born from what the beat uncovered.

"Nothing changed" is itself keep-worthy: a null result recorded is freshness made observable; a null result skipped is silence the next reader cannot distinguish from neglect.

## The occurrence guard

A beat spawns a pursuit only when it uncovers gap-closing work **bigger than the beat itself** — then: a one-sentence acceptance in the moment, provenance recorded (spawned by this tending, this occurrence), designation per the `designate` runbook, and the beat continues or closes on its own terms. Work inside the beat's bounds is just the beat; never dress a beat up as a pursuit, and never stretch a beat to swallow work that deserves its own designation.
