# Onboard — read the project's face before touching it

This is the retrieval protocol's onboarding moment: a project's notebook geography exists so a reader can stop at the right level, and this runbook walks that descent in order.

## The read, in order

0. **Litter first.** A `LITTER-FLAG.md` at the notebook's root, or at the root of whichever repository a session ran in, means a previous session ended with uncommitted changes; settle it per its own text (review the state, form coherent commits or deliberately discard, delete the flag) before or alongside the work. Route-level litter flags are the pass-discipline runbook's business at pickup.
1. **The constitution** — the project's designation instrument, at the opening of its notebook (`Constitution.md` at the notebook's root, which is `notebook/` where the three repositories share one project folder, per the anatomy in `10-work-object-model.md`; a project with no notebook found is not wrapped — see the honesty notes). Take from it: what this project is (identity), what constrains all work in it (bounds), and where its notebook regions, body and inbox live (the notebook declaration). Check that each declared location resolves; a dead pointer is said aloud to the human before any work relies on it. The bounds bind you from this moment.
2. **The aim surface** — `Aim.md` beside the constitution. Take from it: the founding wish as currently sharpened, the envisioned body-state, and what is ripening. This tells you what the project wants now — which is not always what the dispatch assumed.
3. **The registry** — the roster of the project's pursuits (contracts, under `pursuits/`) and tendings (charters, under `tendings/`), `Registry.md` beside the constitution. Take from it: what is live, what is agreed, what is suspended or retired. Tendings especially — their failure mode is invisibility, and the registry is where they stay visible.

## Descend no deeper than the task needs

The face is enough to orient. Go deeper only where the task points:

- Working a specific pursuit → its contract kernel, then its spec and plan.
- Picking up a route or a contract-direct pursuit → stop; that is the `pass-discipline` runbook's binding pickup read, not onboarding.
- A tending's beat → its charter, then the `occurrence` runbook.

## Honesty notes

- A session opened in a body or an inbox first searches for the notebook that claims it, a constitution naming this folder as its body or inbox. No index exists; the search is the lookup. Search at least the folder's parent and its siblings, the host's project and notebook folders the session knows, and, in an existing project, the body's own instruction file, which may still carry topology; a candidate constitution claims the folder only if its declaration names this path, matching folder names alone are not proof. A claim that no notebook claims the folder settles only by naming the folders searched (`rules/40-amnesia.md`). Found, onboard from that notebook.
- If the project is not wrapped — no notebook claims it, the notebook or the inbox is not a repository, a body that has arrived is not one, or the face (constitution, aim, registry) is missing from the notebook — say so rather than inventing a shape silently, and make the offer to wrap first, before any work: name what wrapping would take (plain wrapping, or fusion, absorption, renaming, restructuring — the `wrap` runbook), and ask through the Lead. The offer is non-blocking to answer — a question still gets its answer — and blocking to work: on yes, wrap per the `wrap` runbook (for an adopted body, the geography declaration and the survey of its existing governance surfaces), then serve; on no, stand down, serving nothing in that project. The shared external brain is the sole exception, where work may happen unwrapped. Until his word, captures land on a native surface or one clearly marked file, never nowhere — the locality law applied to a project with no maintained surface yet: land it in the tree it arose in, on one clearly marked file, and port it when the wrap gives it a home. Until his word the project's own pre-existing governance surfaces — backlogs, handoffs, docs — stay authoritative, and no ensemble-shaped structure is invented ahead of it.
- If the face contradicts what the dispatch assumed, surface the contradiction before working; the project's own designation outranks the dispatch's phrasing.
- If notebook surfaces are found nested inside the body — a `docs/` folder, wholly or partly, and even where the notebook declaration itself names those locations — the project is not wrapped as the anatomy prescribes: surface the misplacement as a wrap need before working, through the same offer; relocation is gated work, never silent, and a broken geography is never followed as if lawful.
- The project's own instruction file (a `CLAUDE.md`, an `AGENTS.md`) lives in the notebook, where sessions start; read it, and any a grouping above carries, with the face: whether a session loads them on its own depends on how it was started. A body's own instruction file speaks only about the body. Project-wide words or topology found in a body's file are surfaced as a move into the notebook, and standing words the constitution lacks as a move into its bounds, both on the human's word; in a shared project the file stays as the shared form keeps it.
