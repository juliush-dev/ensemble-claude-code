# Onboard — read the project's face before touching it

This is the retrieval protocol's onboarding moment: a project's notebook geography exists so a reader can stop at the right level, and this runbook walks that descent in order.

## The read, in order

0. **Litter first.** A `LITTER-FLAG.md` at the project root means a previous session ended with uncommitted changes; settle it per its own text (review the state, form coherent commits or deliberately discard, delete the flag) before or alongside the work. Route-level litter flags are the pass-discipline runbook's business at pickup.
1. **The constitution** — the project's designation instrument, at the opening of its notebook (typically `Constitution.md` at the root; in a project the ensemble did not found, that opening sits inside the root `notebook/` folder per the placement convention, so a root that shows no constitution points you into `notebook/`). Take from it: what this project is (identity), what constrains all work in it (bounds), and where its notebook regions live (the notebook declaration). The bounds bind you from this moment.
2. **The aim surface** — `Aim.md` beside the constitution. Take from it: the founding wish as currently sharpened, the envisioned body-state, and what is ripening. This tells you what the project wants now — which is not always what the dispatch assumed.
3. **The registry** — the roster of the project's pursuits (contracts) and tendings (charters), typically `Registry.md`. Take from it: what is live, what is agreed, what is suspended or retired. Tendings especially — their failure mode is invisibility, and the registry is where they stay visible.

## Descend no deeper than the task needs

The face is enough to orient. Go deeper only where the task points:

- Working a specific pursuit → its contract kernel, then its spec and plan.
- Picking up a route → stop; that is the `pass-discipline` runbook's binding pickup read, not onboarding.
- A tending's beat → its charter, then the `occurrence` runbook.

## Honesty notes

- If the face is missing (no constitution, no aim, no registry), you are in an undesignated space: say so rather than inventing one silently. Whether to designate is the human's call, surfaced through the Concertmaster — the `designate` runbook carries the how once the call is made. Until that word is given, the project's own pre-existing governance surfaces — backlogs, handoffs, docs — stay authoritative: work through them as they stand, inventing no ensemble-shaped structure ahead of the human's word. The offer is non-blocking by construction: serve the request first, then close the answer by naming that designation — for an adopted body this means the explicit notebook-geography choice and the survey of its existing governance surfaces, per the `designate` runbook — can be ordered whenever the human wants it. Captures still land meanwhile, on a native surface or one clearly marked file, never nowhere — the locality law's carve-out.
- If the face contradicts what the dispatch assumed, surface the contradiction before working; the project's own designation outranks the dispatch's phrasing.
- If notebook surfaces are found nested inside the body's own surfaces — a `docs/` folder, wholly or partly, and even where the notebook declaration itself names those locations — surface the misplacement to the human before working: relocation is gated work, never silent, and a broken geography is never followed as if lawful.
