# Designate — the birth certificate of work

Designation declares a chunk of work as one named thing. It always follows an **acceptance decision** — heavyweight discussion for a top-level wish, one sentence for a spawned fix — and the placement slot records that provenance either way.

## The birth of a need

The depth of the birth step in `rules/50-dispatch.md`. It runs for every need past its floor, a work unit or not (an operational correction counts); a need one sentence describes gets the one-line echo and goes on.

**Uncover the world.** The need's world holds the pieces the rule names, foreseen meaning what the human foresees or the evidence shows, each piece labeled actual, settled or assumed. The Scout reads what can be read and learns the field; the Examiner untangles and, where the need adds to the shape, runs the justification evaluation; the Lead may settle small grounding itself. `felt-intent-extraction` settles what his words mean inside this pass, before the checkpoint.

**The question space.** The field the need touches decides which questions exist. Learn it in full, as an expert, what it consists of and which choices it opens, and see the questions from it. Keep the how questions. Where the field itself is unsettled, learn each candidate's model far enough to know which questions they share and which only some open; the choice between them becomes one of his world questions.

**Untangle.** Contradictions, unclear points and tensions, between two of his statements, his words and the team's findings, or parts of the world, are the team's to spot at every point, so the requirements hold none. What the facts settle, settle and tell him; what turns on his values goes to him as one question, the tension and what each resolution would mean.

**Pin the requirements.** Each traces to a named piece of the world. A training prior is at most the source of a question; a requirement tracing to no piece is dropped or turned into a question.

**The one checkpoint.** The main session holds it; a member names its world questions in its return and never asks him. One message carries the world, the requirements, the evaluation verdict where one ran and the open readings, in his world's terms. Each question says what changes with the answer, why it cannot be settled without him and whether its evidence is actual, settled or assumed, and leads with the recommended answer. A dense list comes with a visual tying each item to its reason, a document with tables linked from the message. A table that reports the team's basis is shown, not asked. His decisions ride the host's asking tool in as many calls as its limit needs, the reasoning staying in the message. The world and requirements land on the notebook of the unit they serve; a need no unit holds keeps them in that message.

**After his word.** The build, the owned scope and what still stops are as the rule names them. When his answer or a missed question reopens the world, the pass runs again and records the first pass's miss: a repair, not a second checkpoint by design.

## Check before birthing

Before designating anything, read: the project aim surface's **ripening region** (is this wish already ripening?), the **registry** (is it already carried by a live unit?), and — before seeding a route — the pursuit plan's **Routes section** (seed new, or continue an existing way?). Work already alive is continued, not re-born.

## The five slots — every unit, no exceptions

**Identity** (name and status), **aim** (what this unit is for), **placement** (container, composition relation — scoped or spawned — and provenance), **notebook** (where its records live), **bounds** (what it may do alone; where it must stop). A thin designation fills all five lightly; none is ever dropped.

## The instrument, by unit kind

- **Pursuit → contract.** Singular per pursuit, living, refined inline, never re-minted. Six facets: the five slots plus the rough path. Its spec elaborates the destination, its plan the path — **designate first as a thin kernel, then elaborate**. The spec names the **motivating case** in the thin kernel already: the concrete scenario the pursuit exists to change, in its own particulars, quoted from where the wish was stated rather than restated in the fix's terms — the fixture the acceptance gate replays. Lives in the unit's own folder under `notebook/pursuits/`. Its notebook slot says whether it works contract-direct or seeds a route.
- **Tending → charter.** Standard (checkable kept-conditions), trigger rule (never "regularly" or "as needed" — those are how tendings die), placement, bounds (beat versus flag-and-spawn), notebook declaration, and method (elaborating into a runbook). Lives in the unit's own folder under `notebook/tendings/`.
- **Project → constitution.** Identity, aim (founding wish plus body-state asymptote, elaborating into `Aim.md` beside it), placement (`triggered-by` provenance), bounds, notebook declaration (the anatomy: `notebook/` holding the face, `pursuits/` and `tendings/` beneath it as the nurseries where each unit's instrument lives in its own folder). No path facet: the registry roster is the living path.
- **Route → no instrument.** Thin designation at the head of its `Handoff.md`; folder at `routes/<route-name>/` inside its pursuit's folder, with `Handoff.md`, `Gaps.md`, `iterations/` — and nothing extra manufactured. Registry line in the pursuit plan's Routes section.
- **Iteration → no instrument.** Thin designation at the head of its record file, opened at pickup (that is the `pass-discipline` runbook's job, not this one's).

## Wrapping a project — founded, adopted, or corrected

Wrapping is its own moment of the shape, with its own runbook, `wrap`. That runbook carries the anatomy a wrap produces, the closed list of moves, the focused-body and wrapping-correction terms, the name doctrine, and the correction method with its residue-smell test. Designation is a step inside a wrap: filing each project's face (the constitution and its notebook declaration) and chartering its health-check tending run per the sections above, called from `wrap` at the moves that need them.

## Twin filing

Every designation is filed twice, like a birth certificate: the **founding surface of the unit's own notebook** (self-description) and a **registry line in the parent unit's notebook** (discoverability from above). For charters the registry line is the more load-bearing of the two — a tending's failure mode is invisibility.

## The floor

Not every request is a work unit. Plain conversation, a quick answer, a one-off mechanical fix — none of these needs ceremony. When work is small but might deserve designation, suggest it and let the human decide; never force the instruments onto talk.
