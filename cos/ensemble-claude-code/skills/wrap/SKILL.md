# Wrap — putting the anatomy around a project

Wrapping is its own moment of the shape. This runbook produces the anatomy of `10-work-object-model.md` around a body, founded, adopted, or corrected, and it calls `designate` at the filing steps.

## The two entries

A wrap has two doors, and the quiet one is the point.

- **Explicit.** The human orders a wrap read, and this runbook runs.
- **Unattended.** Every member, during any happening, smells wrap needs (`10-work-object-model.md`), the topology smells and the residue smell. A smell autonomously raises a *suggestion* for a wrap discussion to the human. He alone opens that discussion, and a restructure moves only on his word. The watch is always-on DNA; the runbook is what runs once he opens the door.

## The anatomy a wrap produces

A project is wrapped when it holds the anatomy of `10-work-object-model.md` — read it before wrapping: a folder with three repositories, `notebook/` (the face, then `pursuits/` and `tendings/` as the nurseries, each unit's instrument in its own folder), `body/`, `inbox/`. Beside the single project sits the multi-body kind: a pure grouping folder with no notebook, body, or face of its own, each sub-project inside carrying the full shape. Groupings recurse into higher groupings, their members projects or groupings in any mix, per `10-work-object-model.md`.

## The moves and the terms

**The wrap's moves — a closed list.** Initialize as a repository any of the three that is not one; file the face; charter the project's health-check tending at `notebook/tendings/health-check/` (the `health-check` runbook); and **plain wrapping** of a body the ensemble did not found, the smallest form. Then the larger forms, each named to the human at the ask and moving only on his word as a broad restructure: **fusion** (many projects that need to become one), **absorption** (one or many joining an existing multi-body project), **renaming** and **restructuring** of project and grouping folders to match the anatomy (renaming inside a body is body work, not wrapping), and **wiring** (the wrap's own to decide, put to the human at step 7 only when a correction is in flight). A plain wrap's notebook geography is still declared explicitly in the constitution's notebook declaration, and what it declares is the anatomy.

Filing the face and chartering the health-check tending are not this runbook's to spell out: they run as `designate` prescribes (its instrument-by-kind and twin-filing sections), and the `health-check` runbook carries the tending's charter. This runbook carries only the wrap-specific moves.

Wiring — how wrapped bodies come together into the running whole — is the wrap's to decide, always; the Builder's hand edits it. In a solo project that is the whole story. In a project shared with other contributors, the shared wiring is theirs collectively and the wrap never edits it; the wrap instead establishes its own wiring, derived from the shared one by reference — a projection of the shared wiring into the owned — which never overrides it and never enters the projection pushed back. The owned wiring lives as a body of the contributor's own inside the workspace: a focused body whose audience is the other bodies; the grouping folder stays faceless.

**Focused body.** A body that can be reasoned about as a product with one clear purpose, one reason to exist, one target or audience — the audience may be another focused body — independently of what should only have surrounded, scaffolded, neighbored, or platformed it but got mixed in. Un-mixing it moves the scaffolding out into its own body or grouping. That is a wrapping act, never a cut inside a body's code.

**Wrapping correction.** A messy existing wrap corrected by deriving the right shape from what the disk should have held, not from what it holds.

**The name.** A project's name is read from what the body is trying to realize — alone first, then in its place in the grouping, re-judged at every level a wrap forms; the current name may be residue of an unclear vision. A re-judged name re-designates the identity slot, so the constitution and the registry line co-move under twin filing.

## The nine-step method

**The wrapping-correction method** — derived from the blessed meaning of wrapping, not dictated by it:

1. **Trigger.** An unwrapped body, or a wrap that reads as messy — names from an unclear time, scaffolding mixed into a product, a notebook overlaying body-internal governance. Moves only on the human's word.
2. **Read reality, not the face.** Bodies, histories, users, deploy targets, wiring. Read the existing face only to note where it diverges.
3. **The individual question, per candidate body.** What it is trying to realize, read even where the body names that nowhere or names it wrongly, so a name is implied there. Run the focused-body test. Un-mix as a wrapping act where it fails.
4. **The collective question.** What relations hold between the bodies — platform-to-application, neighbor, audience-of. Derive the strategic grouping, recursive, every level faceless. The collective read may sharpen a name found in step 3, never overwrite the purpose beneath it.
5. **Name bottom-up**, re-judged at every level.
6. **Wiring** decided as part of the wrap's design, per the blessed meaning above.
7. **Put to the human as one decision:** shape, names, wiring, moves by form, and what stays open. This is the gate — a broad restructure moves on his word.
8. **Execute with the closed moves only.** Body-internal governance is overlaid by reference, never re-minted.
9. **Verify against the blessed decision, not the plan:** each body passes the focused-body test, the running thing still runs (Examiner work at the gate).

Steps 3 through 5 run a **residue-smell test** alongside them: smell placements and names that are the residue of a quick decision — the nearby option taken because there was no time to think far enough — judged by asking what the far-standing decision would have been (the one that still stands when the vision is clear, not the more ambitious one), never by whether the nearby one works. A long or compound name often marks a thing described rather than named.

## Surveying pre-existing governance surfaces

The placement-and-notebook work at designation includes surveying the body's own pre-existing governance surfaces: list each one, name the notebook role it already fills (registry-like, handoff-like, capture-like, bounds-like), and state its disposition — stays authoritative in place, superseded, or closed — dated where adjudicated.

Those surfaces are overlaid by reference, never re-minted or duplicated: a notebook entry about an item that lives on one of them cites it by that surface's own identifier — a backlog ID such as `B-120`, a handoff name, a file-plus-section pointer — so which item is under discussion stays unambiguous across the two vocabularies.

Twin filing still holds unreduced, per `designate`'s twin-filing law: the ensemble's registry line is still written in the parent's notebook, and overlaying by reference governs content duplication, never the designation's own twin filing.

Thin kernel first, per the thin-kernel-first law (`designate`): the survey starts as lines in the constitution's notebook declaration and becomes its own maintained surface only when it outgrows them.
